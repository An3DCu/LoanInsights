About

Loan Insights works out loan payments, compares two loans and shows what paying extra saves. Everything is calculated in your browser.

How the math works

Every payment is the standard annuity formula, the same one banks and spreadsheet PMT() functions use:

payment = P × r / (1 − (1 + r)−n)

Here P is the amount borrowed, r is the yearly rate divided by the number of payments in a year, and n is the number of payments. With no interest, the payment is simply P / n.

Rounding

Every line of the schedule is rounded to the cent, the way a bank statement is. The small differences that rounding leaves behind all go into the last payment, so the balance ends at exactly 0.00.

Bi-weekly payments

There are two kinds, and they are not the same thing:

  • Bi-weekly (Calculator and Compare): 26 payments a year, each at a 26th of the yearly rate, over the same term.
  • Accelerated bi-weekly (Pay off faster): pay half your monthly payment every two weeks. That's 26 halves, or 13 full payments a year instead of 12, so the loan ends years earlier.

Your data

Nothing you type leaves your browser. There are no accounts, no tracking and no server doing the math.
